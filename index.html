    Rational Methodology

    Bowed: Bass Bar Shift

    The Bass Bar should add stiffness (frequency) with minimal mass penalty. If the shift lowers efficiency, the bar is likely too heavy or poorly fitted.

    Guitar: Bracing Shift

    Switching from Raw to Braced shifts the primary resonance from M2 (cross) to M4 (longitudinal). Precision is critical in the scallop height.
